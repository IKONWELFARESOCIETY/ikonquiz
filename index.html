Examination Guidelines

  1. Students must report to the examination centre before the scheduled reporting time.
  2. Bring your Hall Ticket and valid institute identity proof, if applicable.
  3. Verify your Registration Number and examination paper before entering the examination hall.
  4. Mobile phones, smart watches, books, notes, or other unauthorized materials are not permitted inside the examination hall.
  5. Follow all instructions given by the examination supervisor or invigilator.
  6. Do not communicate with other candidates or engage in any unfair means during the examination.
  7. Any violation of examination rules may lead to cancellation of the examination.
  8. Keep your Hall Ticket safely for future reference.

Fee Payment Eligibility Criteria

Students must meet the minimum fee payment requirement applicable to their course and examination paper.

  1. CA Paper: ADCA – Minimum 30% fee paid from Actual Fee; DCA – Minimum 60% fee paid from Actual Fee.
  2. DCA Paper: ADCA – Minimum 50% fee paid from Actual Fee; DCA – 100% fee paid of from the previous remaining fee.
  3. DAM Paper: ADCA – Minimum 75% fee paid from the previous remaining fee; PGDAM – Minimum 40% fee paid from the previous remaining fee.
  4. HTML & C Language Paper: ADCA – Minimum 100% fee paid from the previous remaining fee.
  5. PGDAM Paper: PGDAM – 100% fee paid from the previous remaining fee.

Note: Eligibility is determined using the student's actual course fee and payment record.
